## Key Reminders

- **Consistency is everything** — 2 blog posts/week, 1 GBP post/week, steady directory submissions
- **Internal linking** — every new blog post should link to at least one city landing page
- **NAP consistency** — Name, Address, Phone must be identical across all directories
- **Don't buy backlinks** — Google penalizes this. Earn them through content and partnerships
- **Be patient** — SEO compounds. Months 1-2 feel slow. Month 3+ is where results accelerate
